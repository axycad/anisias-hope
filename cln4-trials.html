How we will source listings

When listings are added, they will come from public registries such as ClinicalTrials.gov and similar sources. Each entry will be dated, checked against the registry, and passed by medical and trustee review before publication. We may link out to the registry rather than republish full protocols on this site.

We will not invent study names, NCT numbers, enrolment status, drugs, results, or dates.

Search tip

You can search public registries yourself (always discuss findings with your clinical team):

  • Go to ClinicalTrials.gov.
  • Try search terms such as “Batten”, “neuronal ceroid lipofuscinosis”, “NCL”, or “CLN4”.
  • Filter by status (for example recruiting) and location with your clinician’s help.

This tip is general guidance only — it is not a list of studies, and it is not medical advice.

Historic / completed trials
