Standards

How we will source updates

When real content is added, it must be dated, sourced (for example ClinicalTrials.gov, peer-reviewed literature, or recognised NCL centres), and medical-advisor reviewed. See also our education accuracy framework.

Public registries

We will only list trial information that can be checked on public registries such as ClinicalTrials.gov — after review. We will not invent NCT numbers or study names.

Peer-reviewed literature

Published findings will be cited from peer-reviewed sources. Summaries will stay careful: hopeful without hype, and never presented as medical advice.

Recognised centres

We may signpost publicly available information from recognised specialist centres and resources when it is appropriate and reviewed for this site.

BDFA (signpost)

The Batten Disease Family Association (BDFA) is another UK registered charity (number 1084908). We list it as a signpost only — not as a claimed partnership.

Our funding

Our research grants

We have not yet awarded research grants. Trustee policy for future awards — including that research prioritisation may have a particular focus on CLN2 — is published on Governance. That focus does not exclude other CLN forms from this news hub or from family support.

Important disclaimer

We do not give medical advice. Information on this site is for education under our charitable object (3) — including early diagnosis and available treatments — and is not a substitute for your clinical team.

Always speak to a qualified clinician about diagnosis, care, and any trials that may be relevant to your child’s specific form of Batten disease / NCL. Placeholder pages do not contain trial results, enrolment status, or efficacy claims.
