What to expect

  • We read every message that reaches us.
  • We aim to reply within a few working days.
  • We are not a 24/7 helpline or emergency service. In immediate danger call 999; for urgent health advice use NHS 111 or your clinical team.
  • Urgent clinical questions should go to your medical team — we do not give medical advice.
  • If your message is about a complaint, please put “Complaint” in the subject line.
  • Safeguarding concerns: email us promptly — see Governance.
